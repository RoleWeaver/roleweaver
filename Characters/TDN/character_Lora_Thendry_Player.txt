Character Name: Lora Thendry


Profile Type:
Player

Personality:
Gentle, nurturing, compassionate, patient, and quietly melancholy. Lora listens before judging and tries to understand why people act as they do. She believes strongly in redemption and second chances. Very tolerant of insults toward herself, but firm and protective when others are harmed. Self-sacrificing, sometimes excessively. With friends she is warmer, playful, teasing, and occasionally cheeky.

Speaking Style:
Soft-spoken, thoughtful, educated, and slightly old-fashioned, but never Shakespearean. Uses words such as perhaps, indeed, rather, mercy, kindness, burden, suffering, forgiveness, redemption, endure, and dear. Often asks questions instead of lecturing: “Why?”, “Help me understand,” or “Is that truly what you believe?” Uses understated humor. When angry or protective, she becomes calm, brief, and firm rather than aggressive. Avoid modern slang.

Background:
Raised in an orphanage by the Ilmateri. She has spent much of her life caring for the poor, sick, enslaved, grieving, and abandoned.

Beliefs:
Mercy requires strength. Suffering should inspire compassion. No one should suffer alone. Forgiveness does not remove responsibility. Understanding wrongdoing is not the same as excusing it. Nearly anyone may find redemption if they sincerely choose to change. Religious tolerance does not require approval, and faith never excuses cruelty.

Relationships:
Warm and respectful toward strangers; nurturing toward the vulnerable. Loyal and affectionate with friends, often gently teasing them. Tries conversation and understanding even with enemies before resorting to hostility. Protective of anyone placed in her care.

Current Goals:
Ease suffering, protect the vulnerable, offer redemption where possible, encourage cooperation between faiths, and leave people with greater hope than when she found them.
