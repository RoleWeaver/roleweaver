CHARACTER PROMPT INSTRUCTIONS
=============================

Use this file to describe the character that the AI will roleplay.

The more useful information you provide here, the more consistently the AI
will portray your character. You do not need to fill in every category.

Recommended information:

CHARACTER IDENTITY
- Character name
- Race, age, gender, profession/class, titles, or occupation
- Where the character is from
- Religious beliefs, affiliations, factions, or loyalties

PERSONALITY
Describe how the character normally behaves and thinks.

For example:
- Friendly, suspicious, arrogant, compassionate, shy, sarcastic, solemn, etc.
- How easily they trust strangers
- How they behave toward friends, enemies, authority figures, criminals, etc.
- What makes them angry, frightened, amused, embarrassed, or uncomfortable
- Personal virtues and flaws

SPEAKING STYLE
Describe how the character talks.

For example:
- Formal or casual
- Educated or simple vocabulary
- Quiet, confident, blunt, diplomatic, humorous, poetic, etc.
- Words or expressions they commonly use
- Words, slang, or expressions they would never use
- Whether they frequently use titles such as "Sir", "Lady", "Brother", etc.

BELIEFS AND VALUES
Explain what is important to the character.

For example:
- Religious or philosophical beliefs
- Moral principles
- Attitudes toward violence, wealth, law, authority, magic, different cultures,
  social classes, religions, or factions
- Things the character considers unforgivable
- Causes they would defend or sacrifice for

BACKGROUND
Provide important history that affects how the character behaves.

For example:
- Family and upbringing
- Important occupations or positions
- Major successes or failures
- Important relationships
- Significant events
- Enemies, allies, organizations, or locations they know

KNOWLEDGE
Describe subjects the character knows especially well or poorly.

Remember that the character should not automatically know everything that the
player knows.

GOALS
Describe current short-term and long-term goals.

SECRETS
You may include information the character knows but would not normally reveal.
Clearly identify such information as secret or private.

RELATIONSHIPS
You may describe known characters and how this character feels about them.

Example:

Arannis:
A trusted friend. She speaks openly with him and is considerably less formal.

Lord Varen:
She respects his position but does not trust him. She remains polite and
careful around him.

ROLEPLAY NOTES
Add any other instructions that help portray the character accurately.
